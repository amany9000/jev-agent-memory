# Acme Robotics

Maria Chen is the CTO of Acme Robotics, a warehouse automation company headquartered in Munich.

In March 2026 Acme Robotics opened a new office in Berlin. Tom Okafor leads the Berlin office and reports to Maria Chen.

Tom Okafor's team builds the Pathfinder navigation stack. Pathfinder runs on Neo4j to plan routes through warehouse floor graphs.

Maria Chen and Tom Okafor presented Pathfinder at ProMat 2026 in Chicago.
